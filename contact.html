QUICK ANSWERS

Contact Questions

Do you organize expeditions or guided trips?

No. Alpine Ascents is an information portal. For guided climbs, look for certified mountain guides or reputable local operators.

Can you give me personal climbing advice?

We can point you to general information on the site, but we cannot judge your fitness, route or conditions. A qualified guide or instructor can.

How do I report a mistake?

Choose "Correction" in the form above, name the page and describe the problem, ideally with a source.

Can I use your content?

Please ask first. Write to us with what you would like to use and how.
